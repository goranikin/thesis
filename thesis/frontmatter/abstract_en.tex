\keywordalt{combinatorial optimization, diffusion models, reinforcement learning, neural combinatorial optimization, vehicle routing}
\begin{abstractalt}
This thesis studies combinatorial optimization with diffusion-based neural solvers.
Decoding heatmaps into feasible solutions under hard constraints is a primary bottleneck;
we apply decoder-aware reinforcement fine-tuning in the CADO style to align heatmap generation with the decoding process.
Experiments on TSP, CVRP, and MDVRP report optimality gaps, and main results.
\end{abstractalt}
